# GospelCO — Credits & AI Disclosure Copy Guide

Use only the snippet that accurately describes the real project. Replace every bracketed field. A credit line does **not** grant permission or replace a licence.

## General AI-assisted creative
> AI tools assisted with selected visual or production elements in this piece. GospelCO directed, refined and reviewed the final creative. Realistic synthetic elements are labelled where appropriate and as required by the platform.

## AI-generated concept visual
> Concept visual created with AI tools and refined by GospelCO. This image illustrates a creative concept and is not documentary evidence of a real event, location, product feature or partnership.

## AI video / synthetic voice
> Production note: This video includes AI-generated or AI-assisted visual and/or voice elements. Product information and offer details were supplied or approved by the client. Synthetic elements are disclosed through the platform's native tools wherever required.

If accurate, clarify:
> The narration uses an AI-generated voice and is not a recording or endorsement by a real person.

## Stock image / audio / video attribution
> Asset credit: “[Asset title]” by [Creator], sourced from [Provider/source URL]. Used under [exact licence/permission].

Check whether a licence permits the specific commercial use, advertisements, broadcast, client delivery, redistribution, territory, term and editing. Save a copy of the licence/receipt.

## Client-provided assets
> Client assets: Brand names, logos and materials supplied or approved by [Client] are used for the agreed project. Ownership and third-party rights remain with their respective owners unless the project agreement states otherwise.

## Unofficial concept / fan art / redesign
> Unofficial concept artwork by GospelCO. This is a speculative design exercise, not an official campaign, paid commission, partnership or endorsement by [Brand/Club/Artist]. Third-party names, logos and marks remain with their respective owners. Permission or a licence may still be required for particular uses.

This label does not cure an otherwise infringing use. Be especially careful with football posters, club crests, player photos, music artwork and celebrity likenesses.

## Website credit (only when appropriate and agreed)
> Website design and development by GospelCO.

Do not add a public credit if the client agreement or NDA prohibits disclosure.

## Case study / client result
> Project: [Client/project name] — [service delivered]. GospelCO's scope: [specific work]. Published with [permission/approval where required]. Any performance figures shown are from [identified source and period] and are not a guarantee of future results.

## Self-initiated / speculative project
> Concept project: self-initiated/speculative work created to demonstrate a creative direction. Not commissioned by or affiliated with the referenced brand unless expressly stated.

## Internal asset register — recommended fields
- Project and asset filename
- Creator / provider and source URL
- Asset type (stock, font, music, code, AI output, client-provided, etc.)
- Licence/permission and proof file
- Commercial use and client-transfer permission
- Territory, term, account or seat limits
- Attribution requirement and exact credit text
- Expiry/renewal date, if any
- AI disclosure requirements and publishing platform
- Reviewer and review date

## Platform reminders
- Check YouTube's current AI-use disclosure tool for realistic altered/synthetic media that meets its criteria.
- Check Meta's current AI labels/disclosure for Instagram/Facebook/Threads and ads.
- Check current Upwork/Fiverr policies for client preferences, rights to uploaded media, AI use, contact/payment restrictions and order communications.
- Re-check the live policy before each delivery or upload; platform rules can change.
